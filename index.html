The Land in My Dream

Written by Mario Dhewantara at 14 April 2025

As I stepped into this dreamlike landscape, I felt an overwhelming sense of peace wash over me. Endless green meadows stretched before my eyes, gently swaying under the breeze like waves in a quiet ocean. The air was fresh, crisp, and filled with the scent of grass and pine. A narrow dirt path led me toward a small house, its wooden walls warmly aged by time. Around it, a few other cozy homes nestled together like a quiet village. Just beyond them, a serene lake shimmered in the golden sunlight, reflecting the towering peaks that stood guard in the distance.

The lake was still, almost like glass, disturbed only by the occasional ripple of a duck gliding across its surface. Children’s laughter echoed faintly from the far side of the water, where families gathered near the shore, basking in the calm afternoon. The houses, simple but charming, exuded warmth and comfort. I imagined living in one of them, waking up every day to this incredible view. Behind the lake, the mountains loomed tall and majestic, their peaks crowned with snow, untouched and glistening under the sun. The contrast between the emerald meadows and white-capped mountains was breathtaking.

I spent hours simply observing, letting my thoughts drift like clouds over the peaks. Time seemed to slow down here, every moment thick with beauty and stillness. Birds sang sweet melodies from the trees, and I could hear the faint rustle of leaves dancing in the breeze. The whole place felt like a sanctuary untouched by modern life, a perfect harmony between human presence and nature. As I sat on a rock near the water’s edge, I felt truly alive, as though I had stumbled upon a secret corner of the world meant only for those who dared to dream.
